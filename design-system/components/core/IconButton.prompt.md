Circular icon-only button for topbars, card headers and composers.

```jsx
<IconButton icon="bell" label="Notificações" dot />
<IconButton icon="more-horizontal" label="Mais opções" variant="ghost" size="sm" />
<IconButton icon="plus" label="Adicionar" variant="primary" />
```

- `secondary` (white + hairline border) is the default; `ghost` inside cards; `primary` for the single key action.
- `shape="square"` gives a 10px-radius tile for toolbars.
